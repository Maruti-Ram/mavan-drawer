Keylines — why every shape gets a different size

Canvas 24. Live area 20 (2-unit trim). The question is what size each shape is allowed to be inside it — and the answer is not "the same".

1 · Every shape at the same 20 × 20 box

Geometrically identical bounding boxes. Optically they are nowhere near it — the circle and the diamond read noticeably smaller than the square, because a square packs mass into its corners and they don't.

2 · The same shapes at their keylines

Now sized so they carry roughly equal area rather than equal bounds. The circle is wider than the square by 2 units and they finally read as the same size.
