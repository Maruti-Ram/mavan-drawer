Live stroke vs outlined fill

Both panels are the Nucleo plus, taken straight off your disk. They look identical. Move the slider.

Read this first: this page is about encoding — two ways to write the same picture in SVG. It is not about outline-style vs solid-style icons, which are different drawings. plus is one of the few icons where those two styles happen to coincide, which is exactly why it works as an encoding demo. A calendar would not.

1.5
Live stroke

A line, plus a thickness instruction

You wrote a 1-dimensional path — go from here to there — and told the renderer how thick to make it. The renderer does the thickening.


    
Responds to the slider. Weight is a parameter.

  


  

    
Outlined fill
      
A closed region, already thick

    

    
You computed the perimeter of that thick line yourself and handed over a
      2-dimensional shape to flood-fill. There is no line any more — only a blob shaped like one.

    

    
Ignores the slider. Weight is baked into the coordinates.

  





The tell: look at the outlined version's path data. Those
.414 and .336 numbers are hand-computed Bézier curves tracing the
rounded end caps — because stroke-linecap="round" no longer exists to draw them
for you. To change the weight you would have to recompute all eight coordinates and all four curves.
In practice nobody does: you go back to the stroked original and re-export.
