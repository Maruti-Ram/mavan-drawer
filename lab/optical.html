What the compensation is

This is a typeface's side bearings, applied to icons. Every glyph in a font carries a tuned left and right bearing so that when you set text, the gaps look even without anyone adjusting anything. Icons in a uniform centred box have no bearings — they have accidental ones, set by whichever keyline the shape happened to need.

The build now emits dist/metrics.json with the ink box and bearings of every icon. A consumer's <Icon> subtracts them as negative margins, once, and every padding and gap value in the product becomes literally true.
