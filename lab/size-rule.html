At a size we have no master for, which reads better: the smaller master scaled up, or the larger master scaled down?

Each row is one size. The top line draws every icon from the master at or below that size, scaled up; the bottom line from the master above it, scaled down. Left is true size; right is the same icons' pixels, 4×. Recommended to start from: the master at or below, scaled up — and 24 at and above 24. Below 16 there is nothing smaller, so it is 16 scaled down.
