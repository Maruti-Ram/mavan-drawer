# Character — the visual language (O1, in progress)

Explored in `lab/character.html` against Polaris, Nucleo UI and Central Icons
(`node tools/refs.mjs` builds the licensed references locally; they are gitignored).

## Decided

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **No sharp icons.** Round caps and round joins everywhere. | User direction. All three references agree. |
| C2 | **Professional, modern, refined — not friendly or comical.** The "Soft" draft (stroke 2, radius 3, bulbous shapes) was rejected. | User direction. |
| C3 | **Trash:** wide Central-style body hanging from the lid, 4° taper, flat tab handle, two short (4-unit) ribs. | Chosen from the trash explorer. Tall ribs read busy; dots and slots read as faces. |

| C4 | **Trash family:** `trash` (ribs), `trash-2` (rim band), `trash-3` (slot), `trash-4` (two dots). All four use the tab handle and the C3 body. | User picked all four interiors. |
| C5 | **Numbered variants are allowed** (`trash-2`, `trash-3`): the same object with a different interior, offered as a choice. The unnumbered name is the default. | Customers pick a style. This adds to the `<object>-<modifier>` rule; it does not replace it. |
| C6 | **Three masters: 24, 20, 16.** Keylines at 20 keep a 2-unit margin (circle ⌀16, square 14); at 16, a 1-unit margin (circle ⌀14, square 12). The 20 and 16 keylines are proposals until the first icons are approved. | User direction. |
| C7 | **Stroke 1.5 at every master.** | User decision. Foundations §3–§6 re-derived around the quarter grid. |
| C8 | **Size variants for marks:** `<name>-large` and `<name>-small` beside the unsuffixed default. **Step rule:** each size is 12.5% of the canvas longer than the next one down, at every master — 3 at 24, 2.5 at 20, 2 at 16 (`SIZE_STEP` in `tools/masters.mjs`; lint checks every family). A `-large` mark may run to the margin edge; other marks stay under the mark ceiling. `plus`: 18 / 15 / 12 at 24, 16 / 13.5 / 11 at 20, 14 / 12 / 10 at 16. The size suffix comes last (`plus-circle-small`). No `-medium` file: the default is unsuffixed. **Only the sizes with a use case ship:** `plus`, `minus`, `x` have all three; `check` has default + small (a large tick has no job). Lint checks whichever sizes exist. | User decision 2026-09-17: customers want the same mark at different emphasis. Central uses the same three names and the same 24 lengths, but draws only at 24; Nucleo has no size names and instead lengthens marks on smaller grids. Capped at the mark ceiling, large equalled the default at 16, so the step rule replaced the cap. |
| C9 | **Naming order:** `<object>[-<modifier>][-<n>][-<size>][-filled]` — `check`, `check-small`, `check-2`, `check-2-small-filled`, `calendar-check-2`. Each part is more superficial than the one before, so every shape sorts together and each shape's sizes sit under it. **The name is numbered, the style lives in tags:** a shape variant is `-2`, `-3`… (C5), never a word; its style word is written into every file as `<!-- tags: long sweep -->`, built into `dist/icons.json`, and searched by the Library (`check long sweep` → `check-2`). Numbers are assigned in approval order and never reused or renumbered; the unnumbered name is the default. A number marks a different drawing, never a size or style. Composites (`check-circle`, `file-check`) use the default shape. | User decision 2026-09-17: the number is what people see as the icon name; the tag keeps the shape findable. Words in filenames read as modifiers, don't generalise across objects and grow long with size/style suffixes. |
| C10 | **Mark corners use the gentle even bend:** a bent mark (check; next chevrons, carets, arrowheads) gets an arc at the corner of radius **half the stroke weight** — 0.75 on the outline, 1.25 · 1 · 1 on filled (`FILLED_WEIGHT` / 2). That is the tightest bend that keeps the stroke an even width through the corner; below it the inner corner stays a notch and the corner reads heavier (lint warns). Tangent points follow the corner angle, so they sit off the quarter grid by construction. **Exact placement:** a bent mark's outline ink is centred on the canvas to 2 dp (the shift is off the quarter grid too), and its filled ink box equals the outline's to 2 dp — no tolerance. Where the corner is free (check) the filled corner slides along the long leg until its bottom meets the outline's, keeping both angles. | User pick 2026-09-17 from sharp · hint (0.5) · gentle (1 · .75 · .5) · gentle even · soft (1.5 · 1.25 · 1) · rounder (2 · 1.75 · 1.5). Hint and gentle read uneven (radius under half the stroke); gentle even was chosen over the rounder soft and rounder bends. Construction studied from Central's chevrons (sharp skeleton, then one radius per size) — the value is ours. |
| C11 | **Check family:** `check` classic (45° legs, short leg ⅓ of the span), `check-2` long sweep (45°, short leg ¼), `check-3` steep (both legs ~51°, 78° corner), `check-4` lean (short leg 45°, long leg ~53°). All 16 · 14 · 12 wide, `-small` 13 · 11.5 · 10, C10 bend. **Angle exception:** check-3 and check-4 leave 0/45/90 — the tick's character is its angle. | User picked all four 2026-09-17 after rejecting compact and swoosh. |
| C12 | **Chevron family:** `chevron-down` classic (45° legs), `chevron-down-2` flat (~34°), `chevron-down-3` steep (~52°, angle exception); each small · default · large. Widths: classic and steep 11 · 9.5 · 8.5 / 14 · 12 · 10.5 / 17 · 14.5 · 12.5; flat is wider so its legs match classic's length — 13 · 11 · 10 / 16 · 13.5 · 12 / 19 · 16 · 14 (at 20 it steps from 13.5 because a 16.5 large would cross the drawing bound). Up, left and right are rotations of these. **Filled chevrons** match the outline's ink box exactly. A centred corner cannot also keep the leg angle at the minimum bend, so the filled bend grows (up to 2× the C10 minimum) until the angle is exact: classic 1.97 · 1.35 · 1.35, steep ~1.33 · 1.05 · 1.04. Flat reaches the cap (2.5 · 2 · 2) and its filled legs sit up to ~3° flatter at 24, under 1° at 20 and 16. | User picks 2026-09-17: medium width over compact/wide; flat, classic and steep over open (too close to classic) and pointed (reads as an arrowhead); flat widened after it read small at equal width. Large ships: carousels, pagination, scroll cues. |
| C13 | **Sizes × styles is a full grid.** An icon's sizes are decided once from its use cases (C8); every shipped style then has all of them (`check` 4 × 2, `chevron-down` 3 × 3). A style that cannot be drawn well at one of its icon's sizes does not ship — no gaps. | User decision 2026-09-17: a style is chosen product-wide, a size per placement; a missing size forces mixing styles or scaling (which thickens the stroke). |
| C14 | **Optical placement.** Symmetric closed marks (plus, minus, x) sit on the box centre. **Open pointing marks move toward their point**, because the eye reads the triangle spanned by their ends, whose centre sits toward the open side: each shape has an **anchor picked by eye at its default 24 size — classic 0.5, steep 0.61, flat 0.55** — and its other sizes and masters scale from that anchor by ink height (classic 0.32 – 0.6, steep 0.37 – 0.74, flat 0.37 – 0.64). Steep's anchor equals classic's rule scaled by height; flat reads high at that (0.43) and needed more. Up/left/right chevrons shift the same amount toward their point. The shift is declared in the file as `<!-- optical: dx dy -->` (written by `queue.mjs approve` from a candidate's `optical`); lint checks centring against it and the build writes it to `dist/metrics.json` as `shift`, next to the true ink box. **Checks move up** (their end triangle sits low): anchors at default 24 **classic 0.2 · long sweep 0.35 · steep 0.05 · lean 0.3**, small sizes and 20/16 scaled by ink height from their default (0.03 – 0.35). Asymmetric marks are measured and judged by the C15 method. | User pick 2026-09-17 from 0 · 0.25 · 0.5 · 0.75 · 1 at 24, judged in menu rows, inline text and icon-only buttons (square, outlined, circle) at 24/20/16: 0 read high, 1 read low, 0.5 read centred in every context. Flat then picked from 0.2 – 0.65 (0.55), steep from 0.35 – 0.9 (0.61). Checks picked 2026-09-17 on the optical sheet against measured 0.1 · 0.35 · 0.05 · 0.25: classic read low at 0.1 (0.2 chosen), lean chosen between rows 0.25 and 0.35 (0.3). Idea from "Details that make interfaces feel better" (jakub.kr), the play-button example. |
| C15 | **How an optical shift is decided.** (1) **Measure, don't copy.** Each shape gets its own recommendation from a stated measure; a value is never carried over from another icon. For single-bend marks the measure is the **end triangle**: the triangle through both ends and the bend's apex. The mark moves against that triangle's offset from the box centre, by **K = 0.5** of it (`tools/optical-sheet.mjs` computes it). **Ink or line mass is not the measure**: the chevrons' mass sat ~0 off centre, yet they needed 0.5. (2) **Each shape is judged on its own range.** Rows sit around its own recommendation (default ± 0.1 on each axis), plus the box centre and the shipped position; moves in either direction are allowed. There is no fixed grid shared by all shapes. (3) **Always on the optical sheet.** `node tools/optical-sheet.mjs <shapes…>`, viewed on `/lab/optical-shift.html`: a plain page with, per shape, one row per position, showing the mark in its canvas outline at 24 · 20 · 16, a 24 menu row, inline text, 40 square and circle buttons, and 32 · 28 buttons. The shipped row and the recommended row are tagged. Never a chat widget or the Construction board. (4) **The eye decides.** The measure is a starting point. The pick is made by eye at default 24, and the other sizes and masters scale by ink height (C14). (5) **Optical balance is judged in the canvas, against all four edges at once.** A mark must read *optically balanced* — not geometrically even — inside its square canvas (24 · 20 · 16) from the top, bottom, left and right together. **Which axes need balancing is decided per icon, from its shape:** a mark that mirrors onto itself left–right (chevron-down, weighted equally on both sides) is judged only up–down; one that mirrors top–bottom (a play triangle) only left–right; one lopsided both ways (the checks) on both, together, as a grid; one that mirrors both ways (plus, x, minus) is balanced by construction. `optical-sheet.mjs` detects the mirror symmetry and says which axes it judges (`--axes` overrides). **An axis already picked is locked:** a later sheet varies only the remaining axis (`--axes x` after the up–down pick) and keeps the locked shift in every row, the box-centre row included. The sheet varies the judged axes around the recommendation (±0.1 ±0.2 for one axis, a ±0.1 grid for two), plus the box centre and the shipped position, and shows each position **inside its canvas outline** at every master beside the UI contexts. Left–right scales by ink width, up–down by ink height. | User direction 2026-09-17, after a copied 0.5 and a one-grid-for-all sheet were caught, and: optical balance means balanced in the canvas from top, bottom, left and right together — "not evenly, optically balanced"; and: choose the axes per icon — chevron only top–bottom, play only left–right, some icons both. Calibration: the end-triangle × 0.5 gives chevron classic 0.5 (picked 0.5), steep 0.65 (0.61), flat 0.4 (0.55, so the eye corrects it). |

The C3 pattern (wide container, restrained short interior marks, flat handle)
is the reference for other containers: bags, boxes, archive.

## Open

- **Stroke weight.** Proposal: 1.5 at every master (6.3% · 7.5% · 9.4% of the canvas), as Polaris, Central and Font Awesome do. Both are shown in the lab with a toggle.
  2 is the settled value in CLAUDE.md; changing it moves the 16 master and the
  min-gap rule too.
- **Corner radius.** Refined uses 2.5 for containers and 1.5 for bends. Mark corners are settled by C10.
- **Angle exceptions.** Tapers (≤10°) and 30° hexagons are drawn but not yet
  allowed by `01-foundations.md`. The check family (C11) is the first granted one.
- **User icon:** the half-ellipse shoulders with a gently bowed base are
  pending review.
