| C9 | **Naming order:** `<object>[-<modifier>][-<n>][-<size>][-filled]` — `check`, `check-small`, `check-2`, `check-2-small-filled`, `calendar-check-2`. | C10 | **Mark corners use the gentle even bend:** a bent mark (check; next chevrons, carets, arrowheads) gets an arc at the corner of radius **half the stroke weight** — 0.75 on the outline, 1.25 · 1 · 1 on filled (`FILLED_WEIGHT` / 2). | C15 | **How an optical shift is decided.** (1) **Measure, don't copy.** Each shape gets its own recommendation from a stated measure; a value is never carried over from another icon. For single-bend marks the measure is the **end triangle**: the triangle through both ends and the bend's apex. The mark moves against that triangle's offset from the box centre, by **K = 0.5** of it (`tools/optical-sheet.mjs` computes it). **Ink or line mass is not the measure**: the chevrons' mass sat ~0 off centre, yet they needed 0.5. (2) **Each shape is judged on its own range.** Rows sit around its own recommendation (default ± 0.1 on each axis), plus the box centre and the shipped position; moves in either direction are allowed. There is no fixed grid shared by all shapes. (3) **Always on the optical sheet.** `node tools/optical-sheet.mjs <shapes…>`, viewed on `/lab/optical-shift.html`: a plain page with, per shape, one row per position, showing the mark in its canvas outline at 24 · 20 · 16, a 24 menu row, inline text, 40 square and circle buttons, and 32 · 28 buttons. The shipped row and the recommended row are tagged. Never a chat widget or the Construction board. (4) **The eye decides.** The measure is a starting point. The pick is made by eye at default 24, and the other sizes and masters scale by ink height (C14). (5) **Optical balance is judged in the canvas, against all four edges at once.** A mark must read *optically balanced* — not geometrically even — inside its square canvas (24 · 20 · 16) from the top, bottom, left and right together. **Which axes need balancing is decided per icon, from its shape:** a mark that mirrors onto itself left–right (chevron-down, weighted equally on both sides) is judged only up–down; one that mirrors top–bottom (a play triangle) only left–right; one lopsided both ways (the checks) on both, together, as a grid; one that mirrors both ways (plus, x, minus) is balanced by construction. `optical-sheet.mjs` detects the mirror symmetry and says which axes it judges (`--axes` overrides). **An axis already picked is locked:** a later sheet varies only the remaining axis (`--axes x` after the up–down pick) and keeps the locked shift in every row, the box-centre row included. The sheet varies the judged axes around the recommendation (±0.1 ±0.2 for one axis, a ±0.1 grid for two), plus the box centre and the shipped position, and shows each position **inside its canvas outline** at every master beside the UI contexts. Left–right scales by ink width, up–down by ink height. | User direction 2026-09-17, after a copied 0.5 and a one-grid-for-all sheet were caught, and: optical balance means balanced in the canvas from top, bottom, left and right together — "not evenly, optically balanced"; and: choose the axes per icon — chevron only top–bottom, play only left–right, some icons both. Calibration: the end-triangle × 0.5 gives chevron classic 0.5 (picked 0.5), steep 0.65 (0.61), flat 0.4 (0.55, so the eye corrects it). |

| C19 | **The glyph gap.** A mark that is *one character with a detached dot* — `info`'s i, `help`'s ?, later `alert-circle`'s ! — floors the space between **dot and body** at **1 unit** (`GLYPH_GAP` in `tools/masters.mjs`), not §6's one stroke width. That space is the inside of a character, not clearance between two elements. **It applies to nothing else:** not the dot's clearance to the container ring, not repeated elements (C17 has its own rule), not parallel runs. The file declares it as `<!-- glyph: dot -->` (written by `queue.mjs approve` from a candidate's `glyph`); lint identifies the dot by its closed two-arc path, excludes the silhouette (the path spanning the whole ink box), and enforces 1 on that one pair. This **supersedes the ad-hoc exception in C18** (`info-3`'s 1-unit flag-to-bar gap at 16), which lint could not see. | User direction 2026-09-18: §6's one-stroke minimum was written for **simple line marks** — nav menu lines, the `more` dots — where the failure is a parallel run filling in; §6's own corollaries are about enclosed shapes and runs of strokes. Applied to a glyph it leaves **a large hollow space in the middle**, worst at the small masters: at 16 the 1.5 gap plus the 2 dot take **44% of an 8-unit glyph**, a void 0.75× the dot's own size. Evidence: (1) rasterised at exactly 16×16, the separating pixel row reads **100% clear background at a 0.88 gap just as at 1.5** — outline and filled, light and dark — so the extra 0.6 buys no separation (`lab/refs/gap.html`). (2) A scan of all 92 approved icons found **0 of 133 path pairs under 1.5 and only 6 between 1.5 and 1.75** — and all 6 are glyph marks (`info`, `info-2`, `info-3`) or mark-in-container pairs, so the carve-out is narrow and costs the other 127 nothing. (3) References put this gap far lower: **Polaris 0.55, Nucleo 0.82**, both legible. Floored at 1 rather than their values because below one unit the clear pixel row depends on where the gap falls on the grid — the 0.88 test passed on phase, the round cap's last sliver covering almost nothing. **Applied to the whole `info` family 2026-09-18**, immediately after `help`: measured, `info`'s void was 0.8–0.91 of the dot's own size against `help`'s 0.41–0.54, which would have shipped two conventions inside one section of the library. All three were re-cut to a 1 gap — dot, body foot and ink boxes untouched, so every optical declaration still validates. Four `alert-*` icons (circle, triangle, octagon, square) are the same body-plus-dot glyph and take the same gap. **Extended 2026-09-18 to the glyph's own opening.** A `?` is a hook: it starts at the upper left and ends above the dot without closing, leaving a **mouth** at the lower left. Nothing measured it until `selfGap` existed (`tools/geometry.mjs`), because both sides of it are ends of **one path** and lint's pair loop only ever compared *separate* paths. Measured, every question mark in the set sits at **1.33 – 1.45** there at the 16 master (2.6 – 3.5 at 24), i.e. just under §6's 1.5.
