# The judging standard — card 3.1

**What this is.** The thirteen axes a set like ours is judged on (`plans/set-and-rules-audit.md:443-457`), each in the same five lines: what good looks like, in what the eye sees · how it is measured, with the command that reprints it or the surface that judges it · how five professional sets handle it, as *methods* through the transfer test, never their numbers · where we stand, with the key figure and where it is written · what would move it, by proposal id. Everything is cited from the 24 audit documents at the pin **`5933041`** (485 concepts, 2,910 drawings); nothing here is newly measured. The deviations themselves live in the findings register (`docs/audit/findings-register.md`, card 3.2), grouped by the same F-numbers; the proposals go to the decision agenda (card 5.2).

**How to read it.** Each axis carries a two-part grade, **the set · the rules**: the first word says what the 2,910 drawings do (for F12, what the published file does) against what good looks like; the second says whether a written rule states it and a committed check holds it. The words are the same on both sides. **holds** — the set does what good looks like, with named one-off exceptions at most; a rule that is written and checked · **mixed** — part holds and part does not, or it holds for a reason nobody wrote down; a rule that is written but unchecked, or checked but mis-stated · **weak** — the set, or the rule, does not do what good looks like; a rule that is unwritten or wrong · **eye-only** — no tool can settle it, and it is not a rule's business; the surface named is where Maruti judges it. Where two cards measured one quantity, both figures are given with their definitions and neither is picked (the plan's wave-5 rule, `plans/set-and-rules-audit.md:95-116`). The surfaces are the Library's At 16 strip (`lab/library.html:237`), `lab/figures.html`, `/lab/optical-shift.html`, and wave 4's three pages, being built now: the wall `lab/audit-wall.html`, the primitive sheets `lab/audit-primitives.html`, the exceptions sheet `lab/audit-exceptions.html`.

**The picture in plain words.** The set is strongest where one number governs one thing and lint can see it: the sizes (C8's step held to 0.000 in 114 of 114 cells), the container ring (one circle, zero drift), the filled box (1,100 of 1,455 pairs equal to the digit, 1,401 within 0.01, 0 undeclared), the angles (82% exactly on axis, most of the rest deliberate). It is weakest where the product makes a promise no instrument checks: the published optical bearings are wrong for every shape-filled drawing (F12); the gap floor delivers a clear pixel at 16 less than half the time (F8); the margin table makes a square icon and a circle icon change relative size between masters (F2); the badge grammar 38 queued icons will need does not exist (F5). Running under all of it: the drawings keep at least five rules nobody wrote — the scaling policy, the junction rule, the detail rule, the through-air rule, the three radius tiers — which is why the right-hand grade reads *weak* on nine axes while the drawings themselves mostly hold; and the gate holds no geometry at all (`enforcement-gap.md:23-34`). The standard's job is to make those visible so Maruti can rule on them.

## F1 · One apparent weight — set **mixed** · rules **eye-only**

- **What good looks like.** Twenty icons in a 16 px row read as one darkness. A globe is not a black ball beside a thin tick.
- **How it is measured.** Ink area ÷ canvas (never ink ÷ its own box, which ranks a `minus` as the densest icon in the set, `findings-mass.md:476`) and the darkest patch after a blur that imitates a squint. Instrument: `tools/audit/mass.mjs` (`density`, `census`) — a library with no command line; the shape-filled figures need the bypass printed at `findings-mass.md:1133-1155`, which is not a committed tool. Surface: the wall sorted by ink, px².
- **Calibration.** The field judges weight with a blur test against the circle and the square (`references-field.md:359`) and beside the text the icon will sit with (`:369`); Lucide spends the same spread of line on the same concepts — rank correlation 0.92 (`findings-mass.md:23`; a rank correlation of 1 would mean both sets order the same concepts from lightest to darkest identically) — a check that the spread is the concept's, not a target.
- **Where we stand.** The stroke is level (`findings-mass.md:19`); the ink is not — 12.2 to 102.2 px² at 16, 8.4× (`:20`), 1.6× at a squint between the 10th and 90th percentile (`:21`). Two populations: marks at a median 11.1% of the canvas, objects at 21.7% (`:22`). The 16 carries 24% more ink per canvas than the 24, by C7's arithmetic (`:25`). The filled row is 22% Dense by nature (`:26`). No rule speaks about ink quantity, and the card recommends none (`:27`).
- **What would move it.** M-1: no rule, no change — the lever is `-large` marks, not the stroke (`findings-mass.md:464`); M-2: marks against objects, settled on the wall (`:466`); FD-6: state the text each master is drawn beside (`references-field.md:504`).

## F2 · One apparent size — set **mixed** · rules **weak**

- **What good looks like.** A square icon beside a circle icon reads one size at every master; a mark sits where the eye puts it, and the set agrees on where that is.

Placement — `node tools/audit/optical-probe.mjs $P census offcentre should` (`findings-optical.md:728-740`). Corners — `node tools/audit/radii.mjs`. Surfaces: the wall, a square-keyline icon beside a circle-keyline icon at 24 · 20 · 16 (`findings-scaling.md:532`); `/lab/optical-shift.html`.
- **Calibration.** Fluent publishes our keyline table and still reads 8–13 points bigger in the same square, because it spends the margin and a third of its drawings stand past the circle keyline (`references-fluent.md:240`, `:256-264`); the margin as a budget is taken as a question (T4, `:306`), the soft keyline refused because our bearings are a contract (R1, `:331`). - **Where we stand.** *What the eye sees:* every drawing sits exactly on its keyline, and still a square icon shrinks against a circle icon by 5% between 24 and 16 — because the margin 2 · 2 · 1 is not proportional, no drawing is at fault (`findings-scaling.md:458`) — which is why the set reads *mixed*: the drawings obey their keylines, and the pair still does not read one size across 123 and 78 concepts (`findings-scaling.md:456`). Containers' corners agree on three radii; the odd ones are nameable. Marks sit where C14 put them, and 154 other drawings sit a little off centre with nobody having said whether that is right. *The figures:* 280 of 287 keyline-held concepts exact (`findings-scaling.md:21`), **0 of 485** holding their share of the canvas, square : circle 0.900 at 24 → 0.857 at 16 (`:31-32`, S1 `:451-460`). Container corners on the tier value: **61.8 · 61.4 · 58.6%** by card 1.2, radii snapped to the nearest sixteenth within 0.025 (`findings-corners.md:49-51`); **61.1 · 61.1 · 58.3%** by card 2.3's two-decimal count of the same corners (`references-nucleo.md:184`); 93.4–93.8% on one of the three tiers, and the three tiers obey three different laws (`findings-corners.md:49-51`, `:109-113`). 45 concepts carry an optical shift, exactly where C14 was written (`findings-optical.md:47-48`); drawings publishing `shift [0,0]` with their ink off centre: **154** by card 1.11 — the build's 2 dp box, larger axis off by more than 0.005, 166 counting the 12 anchored — and **165** by card 1.12 — `metrics-check.mjs`'s solved box, the offset as a distance, 177 with the anchored (`findings-optical.md:698`); 110 of them nobody has judged (`:59-60`).
- **What would move it.** S1 — hold the share or hold the keyline — is Maruti's eye on the wall (`findings-scaling.md:532`); the container-radius ruling (`docs/09-rule-audit.md:93`) with card 1.2's six recommendations (`findings-corners.md:762-780`); OC-1, OC-2, OC-4, OC-5 (`findings-optical.md:622-679`); R-2, R-4 (`exceptions-ledger.md:731`, `:733`).

## F3 · The armature — set **mixed** · rules **weak**

- **Calibration.** The field credits "one hand" to ownership and a tracked list of repeating elements, not to the grid (`references-field.md:35-36`, `:367`); SF Symbols proves a part is one drawing by counting its distinct outer boxes over every use (`references-sf.md:793`).

- **What would move it.** P7, shared-part persistence as the primitive-drift linter's first check (`findings-grammar.md:656`); the linter's four checks (`findings-primitives.md:1048-1068`); FD-1, the tracked register as the standing instrument (`references-field.md:454`); FM-1, FM-6 (`findings-families.md:591`, `:638`).

## F7 · Crisp where it is used — set **mixed** · rules **mixed**

- **What good looks like.** At 16, at 1×, a straight line is one dark pixel with at most a faint shadow, and the silhouette's edge sits on a pixel.
- **How it is measured.** `tools/audit/pixelgrid.mjs` (`edgeLanding`, `blurScore`) — a library with no command line; every score is in `findings-grid.md`'s appendices; the solid-pixel share is the card's own, uncommitted. Surface: the wall at real 16 px, 1× and 2×.
- **Calibration.** Pixel policy keyed to the stroke — 1.5 on x.25 / x.75, 2.0 on whole numbers — and the outer edge on the pixel while the inner may blur (`references-field.md:357`); the field is split on whether to snap at all (`:376-377`).
- **Where we stand.** A 1.25 stroke cannot be crisp on both edges (`findings-grid.md:17`). Of 312 scorable outlines, 55% have every straight line on one hard pixel and 3% have none (`:21`); 173 — every chevron, check and arrowhead — are outside the instrument (`:19`). 370 of 485 have all four outer edges on pixel lines, 96% of silhouette icons (`:27`); 76.0% of straight length sits on x.375 / x.625 (`:241`). The 16 is narrowly the softest master by solid pixel, 20.2% against 15.6% (`:24`). The filled style is crisper at 16, except 19 line-heavy concepts whose 2.0 lines sit on half-numbers — the worst possible score (`:26`, `:365`). C7 accepted the cost in words; nothing checks crispness.
- **What would move it.** G-1: leave the midline (`findings-grid.md:359`); G-4 is the one avoidable cost, a position not a weight (`:365`); G-7: the diagonals need the eye on the wall (`:371`).

## F8 · Air that reads even — set **mixed** · rules **weak**

- **What good looks like.** Every gap in the set — a cut, a counter, a band between two filled parts, two parallel runs — leaves a visibly clear pixel at the master's own size.
- **How it is measured.** `tools/geometry.mjs` (`selfGap`, `materialGap`, `facingGap`) through `node tools/lint.mjs`, which prints a reading only when it warns. The air card's phase sweep — moving a gap's walls through every position they can take against the pixel grid — and its per-span pixel read were scratch scripts (`findings-air.md:11-16`); no committed tool reprints them. Surfaces: the exceptions sheet for the declared gaps; the wall for the eight concepts below.
- **Calibration.** Fluent derives the floor from the line and holds the knockout at exactly one stroke at seven sizes (`references-fluent.md:284`, `:291`); Nucleo's floor is the 10th percentile of the tightest clear air per drawing — a share of the grid (`references-nucleo.md:42-44`, T3 `:585`); no reference holds anything like 1.5 of air relative to its own line (`references-fluent.md:355-358`). Values refused.
- **Where we stand.** Spans drawn at the floor deliver a clear pixel 67.9 / 65.1 / **41.5%** of the time at 24 / 20 / 16 (`findings-air.md:241-243`), from 5% to 64% by kind (`:259`); 42 spans leave no light pixel and 35 of them pass lint, in eight concepts (`:282-283`), with 83 more near-welds — air that renders 70–99% grey, almost ink. 1.5 is the right single constant — the grid-legal minimum for a 45° channel — and the rule is missing its alignment clause (`:429-430`); a uniform 1.0 floor with no exceptions would flag 34 readings where today's 1.5-with-exceptions flags 74 (`:357-359`). 941 of 2,910 drawings carry a reading at exactly 1.5 (`:374`). The floor's derivation rests on 92 icons and a refuted raster test (`rule-register.md:296`, `:321`).
- **What would move it.** The alignment clause, adopted for new drawings (`findings-air.md:462`, `:501-502`); the 42 spans and 83 near-welds as the cheap subset (`:499-500`); R-5, R-9 (`exceptions-ledger.md:734`, `:738`); the four eye questions at `findings-air.md:828-831`; Fluent T2 (a 1.25 knockout at 16 — Maruti's look).

## F9 · Contact grammar — set **mixed** · rules **weak**

- **What good looks like.** Where one stroke ends on another it lands the same way every time — on the line, not short, not through — at every master and in both styles.
- **How it is measured.** Card 1.7's contact census was a scratch script (`findings-air.md:509`); lint sees 1.2% of junctions and describes them as negative gaps (`:558-561`). The only axis with no committed instrument at all. Surface: the Library's At 16 strip, outline and filled.
- **Calibration.** No reference card measured junctions. The nearest: Fluent lets the air collapse to a bottom decile of 0.09–0.31 where a badge meets its base (`references-fluent.md:348-358`); Cursor adds optical breaks at junctions (`references-field.md:228`). Context, not a method.
- **Where we stand.** 3,036 junctions in 1,332 drawings (`findings-air.md:527-528`); **95.0% land exactly on the other centreline**, the same share at every master (`:585`) — a rule in two sentences a doc could carry (`:606-619`). The mixed part: 63 structural exceptions (`:597`); ten concepts disagree with themselves about the same junction across masters (`:788-791`); the sort family's 18 lint warnings are a contact reported as a gap (`:805-812`).
- **What would move it.** Write the rule down — it ratifies practice (`findings-air.md:833`); R-7, a note for deliberate overlap and a C-row for the sort family (`exceptions-ledger.md:736`); L-2 (`:703`); commit the census as a tool.

## F11 · Restraint — set **mixed** · rules **weak**

- **What good looks like.** Detail dies downward, in a known order; nothing turns to noise at 16; nothing appears at 16 that the 24 did not have.
- **How it is measured.** The subpath census per master (card 1.4 §6, from libraries with no command, `findings-scaling.md:7-10`). `node tools/audit/radii.mjs` prints a per-master radius histogram; the per-concept radii gained at 16 came from a scratchpad dump (`findings-corners.md:7-8`). Surface: the wall sorted by stroke count.
- **Calibration.** Fluent's detail-death order (`references-fluent.md:142-150`), and the rule derived from Fluent and from our own 69 deaths: *a part is dropped when rescaling would take its thinnest side below about one stroke at the new master; a run that cannot hold its spacing drops a member rather than compressing* (`:182-184`, `:195-197`). No system publishes which detail dies (`references-field.md:354`). A detail budget should be measured in line length, not parts (`findings-mass.md:470`).
- **Where we stand.** 453 of 485 outline concepts keep their subpath count; 30 shed, 2 gain (`findings-scaling.md:22`); the budget falls 4.0% from 24 to 16 while the ink box grows 5% (`:270-271`). The mixed part: six concepts gain radii at 16 that 24 lacks — detail appearing at the smallest master, tagged systemic (`findings-corners.md:747`). The heaviest drawings are heavy because the concept needs the line, 0.83–1.16× Lucide's (`findings-mass.md:23`). The rule the set follows is unwritten.
- **What would move it.** FD-3: adopt the two sentences as the rule (`references-field.md:473`); docs/09's missing "detail budget per master" (`docs/09-rule-audit.md:62`).

## F12 · Honest metrics — set **weak** · rules **weak**

- **What good looks like.** Every number in `dist/metrics.json` is true to the drawing it describes, so a customer who subtracts the bearings gets even padding.
- **How it is measured.** `node tools/audit/metrics-check.mjs <pin>/src/icons <pin>/dist/metrics.json` and `node tools/audit/metrics-probe.mjs <pin> [section]`; every figure reprints by the map at `findings-metrics.md:130-140`.
- **Calibration.** No reference publishes per-icon bearings (`references-fluent.md:333-337`); that is the constraint that makes our margin a contract and theirs not, and why Fluent's spendable margin and Nucleo's keyline are refused (`references-nucleo.md:616-619`).
- **Where we stand.** The ink box and the geometric bearings are true to 0.0101 on all 2,910 entries (`findings-metrics.md:37-38`). The optical bearings are not: 1,026 shape-filled entries publish no correction (`:44`); 635 are knife-edge readings — a number that flips by a visible amount when the drawing moves by two thousandths of a unit (`:47`); line-heavy twins read up to 1.63 units apart with one box (`:51`); `paintbrush-4` publishes `null` (`:53`); "13.7–16.2px" was never measured and the truth is 14.00–24.05 px (`:57-58`). The docs promise optical bearings for every icon and the build does not deliver them (`:572`). `alpha` 0.75 moves 1,385 of 1,882 stroke drawings and has never been set against the eye (`findings-optical.md:81-84`).
- **What would move it.** Fixes F1–F8 (`findings-metrics.md:547-556`), three waiting on the rulings in its §12 (`:626-646`); OC-5, `alpha` by eye on a surface (`findings-optical.md:674`).

## F13 · The filled style — set **mixed** · rules **mixed**

- **What good looks like.** Outline and filled share one outer edge; a cut reads as a line; there is one answer to what a ring does when it fills.
- **How it is measured.** Lint's pair check (`node tools/lint.mjs`); `facingGap` on the filled drawings; `node tools/audit/radii.mjs` reads area paths lint cannot; `node tools/audit/refs/untitled-pairs.mjs --ours` and `central-axes.mjs --ours`. Card 1.8 names its library calls and has no reprint map (`findings-filled.md:25-31`). Surfaces: the Library's Filled switch (F); the exceptions sheet for `grows:` and `gap:`.
- **Calibration.** Untitled UI: cut every pair census by what holds each side — slanted end, square-on end, contour (`references-untitled.md:278-279`); a quarter of its solids *are* the line drawing (`:29`), the thing our locked rule exists to avoid.
